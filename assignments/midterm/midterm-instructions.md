Overview
Students will work in teams of 2 to conceptualize a product idea, articulate various aspects of its business model, and design a minimum viable product (MVP) & a plan to test key assumptions related to the product’s success in the market.

The Midterm has four (4) Components and will take place over three (3) Phases. See below for details.

Components
1. Product Articulation
Choose a product idea that solves a specific problem or meets an unmet need
Product Description: Clearly describe your product idea.
Customer Segments: For each target customer segment, articulate their job to be done, pains and gains
Value Proposition: Describe the value proposition for each target customer segments
Competition: Analyze the competition and alternatives
2. Business case 
Build a business case that highlights key drivers of the business and riskiest assumptions
Financial P&L: Identify and quantify revenue + costs, using a logical structure
Benchmarks & assumptions: Clearly document the benchmarks and assumptions that you use
Riskiest assumptions: Articulate and explain the rationale of the riskiest assumptions that you would like to validate
3. MVP
Identify the essential features of the product that can be built to validate the idea quickly
Key Requirements: A list of core functionalities needed to make the MVP viable
Feature Prioritization: A framework ranking features (e.g., by importance and feasibility / cost)
MVP "Prototype": A rough visual sketch or mock-up of the MVP
4. Build-measure-learn Project Plan (single cycle):
Outline the learning plan (build-measure-learn), timeline, and metrics
Learning Plan: Description of the main assumptions being tested (e.g., about customer behavior, market demand, and product utility) and the approach (e.g., surveys, landing pages, prototype testing, interviews).
Metrics for Success: What success looks like for each hypothesis (e.g., conversion rates, feedback ratings)
Timeline: Schedule and key milestones for building the MVP, measuring performance, analyzing and deciding on the next iteration
Phases
A. Product pitch
Each team will submit 2 product ideas, and the rest of the class will vote to determine which product they will work on.

In your group, come up with 2 product ideas. Prepare a pitch presentation for each idea that is 1 minute long. Visuals (e.g., slides) are optional. You must communicate your idea in a very short amount of time (60 seconds!).
Fri, Oct 2, 2026: Present your product ideas in class. The class will vote on which project you will work on.
B. Work on your project
Due dates:

Thu, Oct 8, 2026: Submit slides on your Product Articulation. (2 slides max). Upload to Canvas.
Thu, Oct 15, 2026: Submit slides on your Business Case (financial P&L); call out the riskiest assumptions and why they are the riskiest (2 slides max). Upload to Canvas.
Thu, Oct 22, 2026: Submit slides that define your MVP and rationale for your design choices (2 slides max). Upload to Canvas.
Fri, Oct 23, 2026: Present your product idea to the class (8 slides max). Each team member should have a speaking role. Include the following: (1) Product articulation, (2) Business case, (3) MVP. You do not need to present your Build-measure-learn Project Plan. 
C. Midterm Report
Work on your Midterm Report written submission (PDF submission on Canvas). You may submit your report as a written report or slides, either way is fine (as long as it is a PDF). Your Midterm Report should include all four (4) Components: (1) Product articulation, (2) Business case, (3) MVP, (4) Build-measure-learn Project Plan.
