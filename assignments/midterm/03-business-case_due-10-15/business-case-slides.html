Riskiest Assumptions

Ranked byimpact on the P&L × how little evidence we have

1 · Willingness to pay

Interns will pay $29 when Facebook and RedNote are free

Assumed: 30% of sign-ups pay

30% pay+$34,700
15% pay+$10,400
8.6% pay, break-even$0
5% pay−$5,800
  • Biggest driver of revenue and profit
  • No evidence yet, and every alternative is free
  • Kopa, a verified medium-term housing marketplace, has shut down

TestLanding page with a "reserve your pass" button

2 · Liquidity

Each city has enough rooms for the right dates

Assumed: 40% of members both list and rent

  • A member with no match gets no value, asks for a refund, and won't recommend us
  • A student leaving City A lists their room there, so supply should grow with demand. Untested.
  • Cold start in 4 cities at once

TestSurvey launch schools: where do you go, and will you sublet your room?

3 · Cheap reach

We can reach relocating interns in a 4-month window

Assumed: 15% sign up, $4.40 CAC

  • Users need us once or twice in college, so lifetime value is about one $29 pass
  • One search season per year: a failed channel costs a full year

TestOne ambassador at one school for 4 weeks, sign-ups per dollar
