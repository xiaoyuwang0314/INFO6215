## Pitch 1: Gift Concierge (v2, current)

Target customers: professionals with batch gifting needs, whose work depends on maintaining many client relationships (e.g., finance, real estate, sales). Segments will be refined later.

> If your work runs on relationships, in finance, real estate, or sales, you know the small things matter. A birthday card, a holiday gift, a thank-you after a deal closes. These gestures keep clients loyal and bring referrals. But when you have dozens of clients, they slip through the cracks.
>
> Meet [Name], a gift concierge for busy professionals in Seattle. Upload your client list once, with key dates and a few notes about each person. A week before every occasion, we suggest gifts within your budget. You approve with one tap. We buy from local Seattle shops, write the card in your voice, wrap it, and deliver it on time. Every gift is logged for your records.
>
> Think of us as a flower shop without the flowers. We hold no inventory. We handle everything else.
>
> You focus on your clients. We make sure they feel remembered.

## Pitch 1: Gift Concierge (v1, general consumers)

Positioning: A gift concierge agent for Seattle. Users tell us once who they want to send gifts to and on which dates. We recommend ahead of time, and after the user approves, we buy, write the card, wrap, and deliver on time. We hold no inventory. We sell peace of mind.

> Raise your hand if you've ever remembered a birthday the night before. We've all been there. We care about the people in our lives, but between school and work, gifts turn into a last-minute scramble. What do I buy? Where? Will it arrive on time?
>
> Meet [Name], your personal gift concierge in Seattle. Tell us once who matters to you, their important dates, and a little about them. You can plan your whole year in one go. A week before each date, we send you a few gift ideas. You tap approve. We buy it from local Seattle shops, write the card, wrap it, and deliver it to their door on the day.
>
> Think of us as a flower shop without the flowers. We hold no inventory. We handle everything else.
>
> We charge a service fee per gift, plus an annual plan for people with many occasions.
>
> You remember the people. We remember the dates.

## Pitch 2: Sublet Matching (v2, current)

Business model: Paid membership. Only paying members can post their own listing and view other listings. The platform only handles information and matching. The two parties arrange the sublease themselves, which keeps the platform out of legal risk.

Differentiation vs. RedNote (Xiaohongshu), Facebook groups, Craigslist: international and English-first; verification with school/work email + LinkedIn; matching algorithm focused on rental dates and location.

> Every summer, interns come to Seattle for 12-week internships and need somewhere to live. At the same time, many students leave the city and keep paying rent on an empty room.
>
> Right now they find each other in Facebook groups, Craigslist, or apps like RedNote. Posts are scattered, dates rarely line up, and you never know who's real.
>
> [Name] is a sublet board built for students and young professionals. Every member verifies with a school or work email and their LinkedIn profile. Tell us your dates and where you'll work or study, and our matching puts the rooms that fit your exact window and location at the top.
>
> Members pay a small fee to post and browse. Once you find a match, you arrange the sublease directly with each other.
>
> Real people. Right dates. Right place. That's [Name].

Prepared answer (landlord approval / legal risk): We are a verified listing and matching platform. The sublease itself, including any landlord approval, is arranged directly between the two members, and our terms of use state this clearly.

## Pitch 2: Sublet Matching (v1)

> Every summer, interns come to Seattle for 12-week internships and need somewhere to live. At the same time, many students leave the city for the summer and keep paying rent on an empty room.
>
> Today these two groups find each other in Facebook groups and Craigslist posts. Listings are messy, dates don't line up, and scams are everywhere.
>
> [Name] fixes that. Everyone verifies with a school or company email. You enter your dates and budget, and you only see rooms that match your exact window. When you match, we give both sides a standard sublease agreement, so expectations are clear from day one.
>
> We charge a one-time fee when a match is confirmed.
>
> Verified people. Exact dates. No scams. That's [Name].

Prepared answer (v1): Many leases forbid subletting or require landlord approval. We ask hosts to upload landlord consent, and this is one of the things our MVP will test.
