## Pitch 1: Wellwish

> Think about the birthdays you'll celebrate this year. Your partner, your parents, your closest friends, maybe a few important clients. You want every gift to feel personal. But finding something truly special takes hours you don't have, so it ends up being a last-minute gift card.
>
> Meet Wellwish, a premium birthday gift concierge for people who have more money than time. Tell us once who matters to you, their birthdays, their taste, and your budget. Two weeks before every birthday, we suggest a few gifts picked for that person, like a fragrance they'd love, a designer bag, or a fine watch. You approve with one tap. We buy it, wrap it beautifully, add a handwritten card with your message, and deliver it on the day. When they open it, it feels like it came straight from you.
>
> We make money through a fee on every gift. You pay per gift, and we handle everything else.
>
> Every birthday, a gift that shows you really know them.

## Pitch 2: SummerNest

> Every summer, students move to a new city for a 12-week internship, often far from their school. They need a room for just three months. At the same time, students in that city leave for their own internships somewhere else and keep paying rent on an empty room.
>
> Right now they find each other in Facebook groups, Craigslist, or apps like RedNote. Posts are scattered, dates rarely line up, and you never know who's real.
>
> SummerNest is a sublet board built for students and young professionals. Every member verifies with a school or work email and their LinkedIn profile. Tell us your dates and where you'll work or study, and our matching puts the rooms that fit your exact window and location at the top.
>
> We make money through a paid membership. Members pay a small fee to post and browse. Once you find a match, you arrange the sublease directly with each other.
>
> Real people. Right dates. Right place. That's SummerNest.
