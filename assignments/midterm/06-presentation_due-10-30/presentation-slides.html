R1 · Willingness to pay

Users will pay $29 when Facebook and RedNote are free

Assumed: 30% of sign-ups pay

30% pay+$11,387
15% pay+$644
14.1% pay, break-even$0
5% pay−$6,528
  • Biggest driver of profit
  • Only stated evidence so far: 11 of 30

MVP testFake-door "Unlock for $29" button

R2 · Liquidity

Each city has enough verified rooms for the right dates

Assumed: 40% of members both list and rent

  • A member with no match gets nothing for $29 and won't recommend us
  • Overlap should grow supply with demand. Untested
  • One city pair keeps supply concentrated

MVP test30 verified rooms per city before renters; date-match rate

R3 · Cheap reach

We can reach relocating interns in a 4-month window

Assumed: 15% sign up, $7.50 CAC

  • Students need us once or twice in college, so lifetime value is about $29
  • One search season a year: a failed channel costs a full year

MVP testTwo campus ambassadors with unique sign-up links
