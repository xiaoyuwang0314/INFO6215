## Product Description

SummerNest is a verified sublet marketplace for students and young professionals who move to another city for an internship. Members verify with a school or work email and LinkedIn, enter their dates and location, and see listings ranked by how well they match. Members pay a membership fee to post and browse. The sublease itself is arranged directly between members.

## Customer Segments

### Segment A: Interns moving to a new city (subtenants)

**Job to be done:** Find a safe, furnished room near my internship for exactly the 10 to 12 weeks I'm there, and lock it in from another city before I arrive.

**Pains**
- Can't see the room or meet the person before paying
- Scams: fake listings, requests for a deposit before any viewing
- Regular leases are 12 months; short stays are hard to find
- Listings rarely match my exact start and end dates
- Airbnb for three months is expensive
- Posts are scattered across Facebook groups, Craigslist, and RedNote

**Gains**
- The other person is a real, verified student or young professional
- Dates match my internship window
- Close to the office or transit
- Furnished, so I only bring a suitcase
- Within my budget (often an intern stipend)

**Value proposition:** Find a verified room that matches your internship dates and location, from anywhere, without the scam risk.

### Segment B: Students leaving for the summer (sublessors)

**Job to be done:** Cover my rent while I'm away for the summer, with someone I can trust in my room.

**Pains**
- Paying rent on an empty room, sometimes on top of rent in the internship city
- Posting in many groups and answering many messages
- No-shows, lowball offers, people who back out
- No idea who the person really is
- Time pressure before leaving

**Gains**
- Room filled for the whole summer
- A verified student or intern I feel comfortable with
- Less back-and-forth, faster decision

**Value proposition:** Fill your room for the summer with a verified student or intern whose dates match yours.

### Insight: many users are both segments

A student who leaves City A for an internship in City B needs a room in B and has an empty room in A. One user can bring supply and demand at the same time. This can help with the cold-start problem (to discuss in the business case).

## Competition and Alternatives

| | Verified identity | Matches exact dates | Built for 2 to 4 month stays | Scam risk | Cost to user | English / international |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook groups / Marketplace | No | No | No | High | Free | Yes |
| Craigslist | No | No | No | High | Free | Yes |
| RedNote (Xiaohongshu) | No | No | No | Medium to high | Free | Mostly Chinese-speaking users |
| Airbnb (monthly stays) | Yes (platform ID) | Yes | No (general platform with many other businesses) | Low | High | Yes |
| **SummerNest** | **Yes (school/work email + LinkedIn)** | **Yes (date + location ranking)** | **Yes** | **Low** | **Small membership fee** | **Yes** |

**Positioning (one line, optional for the slide):** The trust of a verified platform at close to free-group prices, built for people who move for internships.

**Main competitive threats to address**
- Airbnb is strongest on trust but expensive for a full summer.
- Free channels (Facebook, RedNote) win on price, so we must show why verification and matching are worth paying for.
